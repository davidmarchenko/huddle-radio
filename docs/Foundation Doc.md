# 1. Executive Vision

Huddle Radio is an AI-native sports media platform that transforms fantasy sports into personalized live entertainment.

Instead of:

* dashboards
* analytics terminals
* score notifications
* robotic commentary

users experience:

* personalized AI talk shows
* emotionally aware hosts
* live fantasy storytelling
* reactive sports conversations
* season-long narrative continuity

Huddle exists at the intersection of:

* sports talk radio
* fantasy sports
* conversational AI
* ambient media
* multiplayer entertainment
* emotional computing

The product treats fantasy sports not as a statistics problem, but as:

## an emotionally persistent narrative system.

The system dynamically generates:

* pregame discussion
* live reactions
* rivalry banter
* fantasy analysis
* postgame recaps
* season-long story arcs

centered entirely around:

* the user
* their league
* their players
* their emotional history

---

# 2. Product Thesis

Fantasy sports are fundamentally social storytelling systems disguised as analytics products.

The majority of emotional engagement in fantasy sports does not come from:

* optimization
* projections
* waivers

It comes from:

* rivalry
* momentum
* bad luck
* emotional swings
* inside jokes
* season-long narratives
* shared suffering
* improbable victories

Current fantasy products optimize:

## information retrieval.

Huddle optimizes:

## emotional immersion.

The product is fundamentally not:

* a fantasy tool
* an analytics product
* a second-screen dashboard

It is:

## personalized AI-generated sports media.

# 8. Runtime Conversation System

The conversation engine is the core technology layer.

The system does not generate isolated responses.

It orchestrates:

* pacing
* interruption
* emotional escalation
* silence
* timing
* memory retrieval
* conversational turn-taking

---

## Runtime Goals

The conversation should feel:

* reactive
* alive
* emotionally coherent
* socially believable

not:

* sequential
* robotic
* transactional

---

## Conversational Hierarchy

At any moment:

* one host leads
* one host reacts
* one host observes

Roles dynamically shift.

---

## Dynamic Speaking Logic

### Minor Fantasy Event

* short observation
* single host response

### Major Momentum Swing

* interruption allowed
* emotional escalation triggered
* multiple-host response chain

### Downtime

* larger narratives emerge
* debates form
* stories resurface

---

## Silence Windows

Silence is intentionally orchestrated.

The system should:

* pause during critical plays
* avoid constant commentary
* allow game atmosphere to breathe

The product should sound:

## socially aware.

# 11. Audio Experience Design

Audio is the primary surface area of the product.

The visual interface exists only to support listening.

---

## Voice Design Principles

Voices should feel:

* warm
* conversational
* grounded
* slightly imperfect

Avoid:

* announcer voices
* excessive enthusiasm
* exaggerated realism

---

## Conversational Naturalism

Hosts should:

* occasionally overlap
* hesitate
* abandon thoughts
* laugh softly
* interrupt naturally

Minor imperfections create:

## presence.

Perfect delivery feels artificial.

---

## Audio Layering

The system dynamically balances:

* game audio
* crowd noise
* host dialogue
* emotional emphasis

During major plays:

* hosts briefly pull back
* crowd noise rises
* reaction follows naturally
